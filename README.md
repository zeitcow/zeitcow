# Jean C. Martinez

Litigation paralegal and independent researcher working at the intersection of **AI systems, legal evidence, and software development**. I build and contribute to tools for source-grounded legal research, agent reliability, provenance, and auditable workflows.

My interests include AI and agentic-system discovery, preserving execution evidence, reproducible evaluations, legal research infrastructure, and responsible use of AI in legal practice.

## Research

**[Discovering Agentic AI: Discovery, Preservation, and Proof in AI and Agentic Systems](https://osf.io/preprints/lawarchive/5bgea_v2)**  
*Independent white paper · Law Archive (OSF Preprints) · 2026*

Examines how evidence from AI and agentic systems can be identified, preserved, reconstructed, and evaluated in the context of U.S. civil discovery and evidentiary practice.

## Selected projects

- **[Implicit Core](https://github.com/zeitcow/implicit-core)** — Python infrastructure for addressable, versioned AI-agent experiences, selective state materialization, provenance, and reproducible evaluation. Published as [`implicit-ai`](https://pypi.org/project/implicit-ai/).
- **[Pro-One](https://github.com/pro-one-org/pro-one)** — Development-stage, source-grounded legal research and procedural intelligence, with versioned evidence, citation checks, and human-review boundaries. Not a live legal advice service.
